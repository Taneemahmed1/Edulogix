EMPLOYER-SPONSORED VISAS

Have an Australian Employer Ready to Sponsor You?

Employer-sponsored pathways can provide eligible skilled workers with an opportunity to work in Australia with employer sponsorship.

Explore Employer-Sponsored Visa Options
Subclass 186 Permanent Residency

Employer Nomination Scheme — Subclass 186

A direct pathway to Australian permanent residency for skilled workers nominated by an approved Australian employer. Available under Direct Entry and Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) streams.

PR Pathway • No Points Test
Subclass 482 Temporary Work / TSS

Skills in Demand Visa — Subclass 482

For eligible skilled professionals sponsored by an Australian employer to fill genuine skill shortages. Work full-time with clear routes transitioning to permanent residency after 2 years.

Up to 4 Years • Employer Sponsored
Subclass 494 Regional Employer

Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional — Subclass 494

Enables regional employers to address identified labor shortages by sponsoring skilled workers. Offers a 5-year provisional visa with a direct pathway to permanent residence via Subclass 191.

5-Year Regional • 191 PR Pathway
Subclass 407 Occupational Training

Training Visa — Subclass 407

For applicants seeking to participate in workplace-based occupational training activities in Australia to enhance their skills for their current occupation, tertiary study, or field of expertise.

Up to 2 Years • Skill Enhancement
