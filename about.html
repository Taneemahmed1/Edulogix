5-YEAR IMPACT PLEDGE

Giving Back to the Country You Call Home

To mark our 5-year business milestone and the expansion of Edulogix Migration, we are embedding community purpose directly into our growth.

Through the Edulogix Impact Pledge, 5% of our professional service fee revenue is donated to ACNC-registered Australian charities supporting healthcare access, nursing welfare, and regional communities.
