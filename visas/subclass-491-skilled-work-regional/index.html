Subclass 491: Skilled Work Regional Visa

A 5-year provisional visa providing 15 bonus points to live, work, and study in regional Australia — with a direct permanent residency pathway through Subclass 191.

Check eligibility ↓
Eligibility

Nominated by a state/territory government or sponsored by an eligible family member in regional Australia, scoring 65 points or more.

Processing Time

Processing times vary by pathway (state nomination vs family sponsorship), occupation, and documentation completeness.

Government Fee

From AUD $6,140 for the main applicant, plus additional charges for family members.

Stay Duration

5 years provisional residence, with a direct pathway to permanent residency (Subclass 191) after 3 years.

Overview

The Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (Subclass 491) lets skilled workers and their families live, work, and study in designated regional areas of Australia for up to 5 years.

Visa holders enjoy full work rights, unrestricted international travel, and the ability to enroll in vocational courses or higher education. Uniquely for a provisional visa, 491 holders and accompanying family members can enrol in Medicare across Australia from the date of visa grant.

Key Features

🗺

Five-Year Regional Stay

Live, work, and study in any designated regional area for 5 continuous years with full flexibility.

🛡

Direct Pathway to PR (191)

Transition to the permanent Subclass 191 visa after 3 years, subject to regional residence compliance.

✨

15 Extra Points Boost

Receive an immediate 15 points boost through either state nomination or eligible family sponsorship.

👪

Full Family Inclusion

Include your spouse, de facto partner, and dependent children on your application with full rights.

Key Eligibility Requirements

Requirement 1

Age Limit

  • You must be under 45 years of age at the time of invitation.
  • Turning 45 after receiving your invitation does not affect eligibility, provided you lodge within the 60-day window.
Requirement 2

Nominated Occupation & Skills Assessment

  • Your occupation must be listed on the applicable regional skilled occupation list.
  • You must hold a positive, valid skills assessment issued within the 3 years preceding your invitation date.
  • Medical practitioners with unconditional board registration and legal practitioners admitted in an Australian jurisdiction meet this requirement through their professional licensing.
Requirement 3

English Language Proficiency

  • The primary applicant must demonstrate at least Competent English at time of invitation (passport exemption or approved test like IELTS 6.0 each band, PTE 50 each band).
  • Accompanying adult family members (18+) must show at least functional English or pay the second instalment charge.
Requirement 4

Points Test Threshold (65+ Points)

  • You must achieve at least 65 points at Expression of Interest stage (including the 15 points boost).
  • Every point claimed must be proven when the invitation arrives: age, qualifications, experience, regional study, community language, and partner skills.
Requirement 5

Nomination or Family Sponsorship

  • Pathway A: Nominated by an Australian state or territory government agency.
  • Pathway B: Sponsored by an eligible relative (18+, Australian citizen/PR/eligible NZ citizen, usually resident in a designated regional area). Eligible relatives include: parent, child, sibling, aunt/uncle, niece/nephew, grandparent, or first cousin.

Health, Character and Background Checks

🩺

Health Clearances

All applicants and certain non-accompanying family members must complete immigration medical examinations with approved panel physicians.

📜

Police Clearances

You and all family members aged 16+ must provide police certificates for Australia (if resident 12+ months) and all overseas countries lived in for 12+ months in the last 10 years.

✅

Integrity & Debts

Genuine documentation is compulsory. You and all family members must also have cleared any debts owed to the Commonwealth government.

Application Process

1

Obtain a positive Skills Assessment

Secure a formal assessment in your nominated regional occupation.

2

Complete approved English test

Prove competent English proficiency before submitting your EOI.

3

Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) via SkillSelect

Declare your points breakdown and nominate your target regional pathway.

4

Secure state nomination or family sponsorship

Apply to the state government or lodge sponsorship evidence with an eligible relative.

5

Receive an official invitation to apply

Upon invitation, you have 60 calendar days to submit your complete application.

6

Lodge your application via ImmiAccount

Attach all identity, employment, qualification, regional, and character documents.

7

Visa grant & relocate to designated regional Australia

Begin your 5-year stay and work towards your Subclass 191 permanent residency.

Travel and Visa Conditions

  • Regional Residence and Work Mandate: You and all family members must live, work, and study exclusively in designated regional areas — major metropolitan centres Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane are strictly excluded.
  • Address and Employment Reporting: You must notify the Department within 28 days of any change to your residential address, contact details, employer, or work location.
  • Unrestricted International Travel: Free travel in and out of Australia while the 5-year provisional visa remains in effect.
