Australian Migration FAQs

The appropriate visa pathway depends on your individual circumstances, including your occupation, qualifications, work experience, age, family circumstances, sponsorship and other eligibility requirements. A consultation can help you understand which pathways may be relevant to you.
Some Australian visa pathways do not require an employer sponsor, while others do. Skilled migration pathways may have different nomination and eligibility requirements.
